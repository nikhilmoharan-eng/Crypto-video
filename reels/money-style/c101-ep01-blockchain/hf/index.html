Crypto 101.
Episode ek.
Blockchain.
Sab bolte hain, par hai kya?
Socho ek diary hai.
Har page pe likha hai, kisne kisko kitna paisa bheja.
Har page ko kehte hain, ek block.
Aur har naya page, pichhle page se juda hota hai.
Isliye naam hai, blockchain.
Ab twist.
Ye diary kisi ek ke paas nahi hai.
Hazaaron computers ke paas, iski same copy hai.
Agar koi ek page badalne ki koshish kare,
toh baaki hazaaron copies match nahi karengi.
Aur network, us badlaav ko reject kar dega.
Isliye blockchain pe likhi entry, mitayi nahi ja sakti.
Toh yaad rakho:
blockchain matlab, ek shared diary.
Koi bank nahi,
koi boss nahi,
sab ke paas same record.
Agla episode:
aapka crypto, wallet mein hota hi nahi.
Follow karo.
